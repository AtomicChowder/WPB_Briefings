Broker AI is commoditising research from below — the private bank's differentiator moves to judgement and execution

Hong Kong's next generation is converting property into bankable assets — an addressable-wallet expansion, if capacity exists

Hong Kong's next-generation wealthy are moving out of physical property and into deposits, hedge funds and other financial assets, driven by liquidity and the far greater ease of passing financial assets between generations; advisers report the switch happening at the point of inheritance, not the point of investment. For Adam the constraint is execution, not demand: capturing an inheritance-triggered rotation means onboarding, suitability and product shelf can absorb lumpy, event-driven inflows on the family's timetable rather than the bank's — which is a change-delivery question about capacity and journey readiness, not a proposition question.
