HSBC weighs merging Singapore's wholesale, retail and private banking units into one entity

HSBC is examining folding its Singapore operations — split today between the locally incorporated HSBC Bank (Singapore) and the branch of The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation — into a single entity, Bloomberg reports. The move sits inside Georges Elhedery, HSBC Group CEO's wider simplification drive, which the bank lifted to a $2.0bn cost-savings target at its August results, though no timetable has been set. Hong Kong regulators wave through 36 agentic-AI pilots spanning banking, payments and wealth

The HKMA, alongside the SFC, Insurance Authority and MPFA, selected 36 agentic-AI use cases from about 100 proposals for its expanded GenA.I. Sandbox++, covering 30 financial institutions and 27 technology partners across onboarding, payments and customer-interaction workflows. HSBC isn't among the disclosed pilots — HKT Payment and Ant Bank are — but the sandbox's rules on autonomy and eventual commercial sign-off will govern how fast any bank in Hong Kong, HSBC included, can move agentic AI from pilot to production. For Adam, that's the regulatory scaffolding his own AI-enabled change agenda has to build against.

Hong Kong wealthtech Growhill switches on an AI workforce for independent advisors

Growhill Wealth, rebranded from GROW Digital Wealth, activated an agentic AI workforce for its roughly 200 independent financial advisors across Asia, with Alan Lau, Executive Chairman of Growhill Wealth pitching it as private-bank-grade AI tooling "at a fraction of traditional platform costs." It's a small platform (~$800m across the network), but a clean early signal of AI-native entrants undercutting the cost of tooling that HSBC's own RMs already rely on. For Adam, it's a bellwether on mass-affluent margin pressure worth tracking, not yet a scale threat.
